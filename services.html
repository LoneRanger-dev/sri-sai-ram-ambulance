A/C ICU Ambulance

Every SSR vehicle is a fully-equipped, air-conditioned ICU ambulance — not an optional upgrade. That means a stretcher, oxygen supply, cardiac monitoring points and a stable, cool cabin are standard on every single dispatch across Visakhapatnam, whether it's a short local transfer or a longer inter-city trip. A/C comfort matters most on longer transfers and in Visakhapatnam's heat and humidity, keeping patients stable and families reassured throughout the journey.

Ventilator Support

Patients who need uninterrupted respiratory support during transport are carried with an on-board ventilator and continuous monitoring, attended throughout the transfer. This is standard equipment on every SSR ambulance, so there's no delay arranging a ventilator-capable vehicle in an emergency — the ambulance that reaches you already has it fitted and ready to use.

Freezer Box Service

SSR provides dignified, hygienic freezer box transport, available 24 hours a day for both local moves within Visakhapatnam and longer inter-city journeys. The freezer box is fitted in the same vehicle used for emergency dispatch, so families can count on the same careful, respectful handling and prompt response at any hour, including nights and festival holidays.
